### PUBLIC NOTICE

Notice is hereby given that the Williamson County Election Commission will convene in person on **Friday, September 11, 2026 at 1:00 p.m.** at the Williamson County Election Commission Office located at 405 Downs Blvd, Franklin. **Time for Public Comment is provided during the meeting.**

For more information, please email Chad Gray, Administrator of Elections at chad.gray@williamsoncounty-tn.gov

Elections and Voting
The Town of Thompson's Station electorate includes a Mayor and four Town Aldermen. They comprise the 
Board of Mayor and Alderman
 (BOMA). The Board is responsible for establishing Town policies and procedures by considering and passing ordinances and/or resolutions that, in turn, are implemented by appropriate Town departments.
The Town votes to elect two aldermen every two years to serve four year terms. The mayoral election cycle is every four (4) years. Elections take place in November on those years.

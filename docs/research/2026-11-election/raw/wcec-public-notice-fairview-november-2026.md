PUBLIC NOTICE
NOTICE OF ELECTION
CITY OF FAIRVIEW MUNICIPAL ELECTION
TUESDAY, NOVEMBER 3, 2026

Notice is hereby given that a **City of Fairview Municipal Election** will be open and held in all
the applicable Election Day Voter Center locations in Williamson County, Tennessee, on
Tuesday, November 3, 2026. The following offices are to be elected:

**Mayor and Two (2) Commissioners**

In order to qualify as candidate for the municipal offices above, qualifying petitions must be
filed in the Election Commission office no later than Thursday, August 20, 2026, 12:00 Noon,
prevailing time (T.C.A.§ 2-5-101(a)). Municipal candidates must also meet all requirements as
required by the municipal charter.

**Withdrawal Deadline**

On or before August 27, 2026, 12:00 Noon, prevailing time, candidates may withdraw their
names from the official ballot: request is to be in writing, signed by the candidate, and submitted
to the Election Commission Office. (T.C.A. § 2-5-204 (b) (1))

**VOTER REGISTRATION DEADLINE**

The deadline to register to vote is **Monday, October 5, 2026, 4:30 P.M.** Voter registration
forms can be obtained at the Tennessee Department of Safety Driver Testing Centers, the
Department of Human Services, the Williamson County Health Department, and libraries within
Williamson County. Voter registration may also be completed online at
https://ovr.govote.tn.gov/

Voter registration forms postmarked no later than Monday, October 5, 2026, will be processed
for this election. Persons already registered in Williamson County may have their permanent
voter registration records altered upon receipt of a written notification, over the voters' signature,
on or before Thursday, October 29, 2026. Each person who registers by mail or online shall
appear in person to vote in the first election the person votes in after such registration becomes
effective. (T.C.A. § 2-2-115 (7))

The Election Commission Office will be closed on
Monday, September 7, 2026, in observance of Labor Day.

The Election Commission Office is located at 405 Downs Blvd, Franklin, Tennessee, 37064.
Office hours are as follows:
8:00 A.M to 4:30 P.M
Monday-Friday

For further information contact Chad Gray, Administrator of Elections, at telephone number
(615) 790-5711 or chad.gray@williamsoncounty-tn.gov

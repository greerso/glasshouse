Agenda-Election Commission-Friday, August 21, 2026-1:00 P.M.

I. Call to Order
II. Pledge of Allegiance
III. Public Comment (Pursuant to Public Chapter 300)
IV. Old Business
    (1) Approve the minutes from the June 5, 2026, Meeting
    (2) Continuing Discussion Relative to Adopting a Policy on Placing Qualified Names of Candidates on the Ballot by the Election Commission (T.C.A. § 2-5-204) pertinent to Reasonable Timeframes for a Quasi-Judicial Hearing
V. New Business
    (1) Certify the election results of the August 6, 2026, Federal & State Primary & State & County General Election
    (2) Accept the Call for Elections for the City of Fairview, Town of Nolensville and Town of Thompson Station for Municipal Offices appearing on the November 3, 2026, State General Election ballot
    (3) Discuss Early Voting Locations, Dates & Hours for the upcoming November 3, 2026, State General and Municipal Elections
    (4) Set the date for the post-election performance audit for the August 6, 2026, Federal/State Primary & County General Election
VI. Other Business (No Action to be Taken)
VII. Adjournment

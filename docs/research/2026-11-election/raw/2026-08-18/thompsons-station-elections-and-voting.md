# Elections and Voting (body)

The next election will take place in November, 2026.

On November 7th, 2022 the Town elected Brian Stover to serve as Mayor for a four (4) year term. The Town also voted to elect Bob Whitmer to a four (4) year term as Town Aldermen.

BOMA Members: Brian Stover (Mayor), Shaun Alexander (Vice Mayor), Bob Whitmer, Kreis White, Harry King.

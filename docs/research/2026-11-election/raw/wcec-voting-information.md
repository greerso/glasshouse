# Voting Information

## Early Voting
- Early voting begins (20) days prior and usually ends (5) days prior to Election Day
- Any registered voter in the county may vote at any established early voting location regardless of their registered address
- Early voting locations and hours are determined for each election approximately six weeks prior to Election Day
- Linked document: Early Voting Information-August 6, 2026 Federal/State Primary & County General Election

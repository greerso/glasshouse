public safety investment, historic funding for the first time, reaching up to a million dollars for
public safety, expanded services, 100% increase for sheriff services, a new fire captain position,
continued support for dispatch services, animal control and rescue squad station 23. Our
operations and personnel with strategic staffing, adding critical roles in HR, codes, public works,
and wastewater.
